Learn About Scams

🎣 Phishing Scams

Fake messages or websites designed to steal personal information.

Stay safe: Check links and verify messages independently.

🛍 Fake Online Stores

Fraudulent shops may advertise products at unbelievable prices.

Stay safe: Research the seller and use protected payment methods.

💼 Job Scams

Fraudsters may offer fake jobs and demand advance payments.

Stay safe: Verify the employer and never pay to secure a job.

📈 Investment Scams

Fake investments may promise guaranteed or unusually high returns.

Stay safe: Check registration and never trust guaranteed profits.

👤 Impersonation Scams

Scammers pretend to be bank staff, officials, friends, or relatives.

Stay safe: Contact the person using a number you already trust.

📦 Marketplace Scams

Fake buyers or sellers may send false payment confirmations or links.

Stay safe: Verify payments in your official account.
